Some Friday AI fun:

Can a gorilla ride a camel? This is the title of a paper published two days ago by researchers from McGill and Microsoft that aims to build an AI model that can answer these types of “plausibility questions” from text alone.

One of the model presented claims that a gorilla could indeed ride a camel.

I wonder if by feeding the model data from fiction, you could answer questions like “Would Batman win in a fight against Spider-Man?”
