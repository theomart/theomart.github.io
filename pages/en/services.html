Agentic setup audit

€1,500 excl. VAT per day, fixed price, two to four days

I take your repo and look at what actually happens when an agent works in it, which CLAUDE.md loads, which hook fires, and what stops applying as soon as the agent goes through Bash instead of its editing tools. The first move is to strip everything out, CLAUDE.md, skills, hooks, and see where the agent fails on its own before deciding what to put back. Most of the work is async, with two or three short interviews, so it does not tie up the team.

  • The CLAUDE.md files, the skills, the hooks, the permissions, and who owns them
  • The settings set at team level that do not do what everyone assumes, a key versioned in the repo can be silently ignored, no error, no message
  • What gets checked before a merge, and review when it comes from an agent, which sometimes gives a verdict on a whole PR after checking only part of it
  • Token cost, where it goes, and the spread between your developers on the same work
  • What all of it changes, or does not, in what you ship

At the end you have a written playbook, specific to your repo, with a 90-day plan and an hour to walk through it. The document stays with you and is built to be run without me.

Workshop in your own repo

€2,500 excl. VAT per day, billed by the day and never per head

One day on site or remote, on your own code, with your real tickets. No sample project and no sandbox, what we write during the day is committed to your repo that evening. The hour by hour format and the questions I get before a booking are on the Claude Code training page.

  • We take three or four tickets from the current sprint and push them through the agent together
  • We write the repo’s CLAUDE.md files during the session, and your developers write them, not me: according to a recent study of public repos, a rule that lands in a CLAUDE.md rarely comes back out, so it had better have an author who stays
  • We wire up the hooks and skills that are useful at your company and throw out the ones that are not
  • We look at what it costs, in tokens and in review time

With eight developers in the room the day lands a little over €300 a head, and that price does not move if you put twelve in.

Who this is for, and who it is not for

It works when the agents have been in the repo for a few months, the licences are paid for, and nobody can say what changed in what ships.

Four situations where calling me is a waste of your money

  • You have not put an agent in the repo yet. There is nothing to audit, take the workshop day, it costs less and it sets up what there will be to audit later.
  • You are looking for OPCO funding or a Qualiopi-certified training body, the French schemes. I am not one, the engagement comes out of the team’s budget, like consulting.
  • You are looking for one more developer on the team, as staff augmentation, for several months. The build format exists for shipped code on a written scope, not to fill a seat.
  • You want a usage dashboard to show a steering committee. Claude Code already exports its line and token counters through OpenTelemetry, and nothing in those counters tells an agent looping on nothing apart from real output. What I look at is time to merge on code that passes review.

Compared to a Claude Code training course

Serious Claude Code courses exist in France, and when a team is discovering the tool they are the right format. The difference with what I do is one of kind: I turn up in your repo, on your tickets, and what comes out of the day stays there. I went through their public prices and programs in August 2026 in a separate piece, along with what that method is worth and what it does not prove.

None of the formats has a tool in its name, and that is deliberate. I work mostly with Claude Code, but what I maintain is the harness, and it carries over. At a large company the choice of model vendor is made very high up and can change mid-year for reasons that have nothing to do with engineering, and what was written into the repo has to survive that.
