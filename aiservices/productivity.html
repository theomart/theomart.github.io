AI Tools That Actually Get Used

Your team has ChatGPT. They're still drowning in meetings.

Why It Matters: You bought Microsoft Copilot licenses. Maybe ChatGPT Enterprise. Your team uses them to write emails slightly faster. Meanwhile, they're still burning 15 hours a week on status meetings, manual reports, and inbox archaeology. Microsoft's own data shows 70% of Copilot users save time—but most organizations capture less than 10% of potential value.

What We Do:

  • Workflow Autopsy (2 weeks): Shadow your teams. Find where time dies. Map which AI tools could actually help. Skip the 200-slide deck—deliver a hit list of quick wins.
  • Practical Training That Sticks: Not "here's how ChatGPT works." More like "here's the exact prompt that turns your 3-hour board deck into 30 minutes." Custom prompt libraries for your actual workflows.
  • Zero-IT Implementation: No new software. No IT tickets. Just smart configurations of tools you already own—Copilot, ChatGPT, Notion, Google Workspace. If it requires a developer, we don't do it.
  • Adoption Tracking: Usage dashboards at 30/60/90 days. Know exactly who's saving time and who's still doing things the old way. Real metrics, not feel-good surveys.

  • Sales Team: RFP responses in 2 hours instead of 2 days. Same win rate, 10× faster.
  • Marketing: Campaign reports that write themselves. 6 hours → 45 minutes.
  • Engineering: Design docs generated from meeting transcripts. Never miss a requirement again.
  • Finance: Monthly close summaries in minutes. Variance explanations that don't suck.
  • Everyone: Meeting notes that capture decisions, not just discussion. Action items that actually get tracked.
